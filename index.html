Questions, answered

Anything else, just send me a message.

What size dogs do you groom?

Small and medium dogs at the moment. If you're not sure where your dog fits, send me their breed and I'll let you know.

What do I need to provide?

Just a space to work, a plug socket and a little water. I bring all my own grooming equipment and products, and I clean up afterwards.

Is it suitable for nervous or older dogs?

Yes. There's no car journey, no waiting room and no other dogs, so they stay calmer at home. I take things slowly and work at their pace.

How often should my dog be groomed?

Most dogs do well every 6–8 weeks. I can book you a regular slot so you don't need to remember.

Do you have a salon?

No, I'm fully mobile for now and groom every dog in their own home.

What if I need to cancel?

Please give at least 24 hours' notice. Cancellations or changes with less notice, and missed appointments, are charged at the full price.
